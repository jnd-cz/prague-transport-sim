## 3. Pedals, coasting, braking

| ID | Fact | Variant, source | Game now | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| T3-P1 | Two pedals: drive and brake. | all; ucebnice p. 4 | pedal-style controller J1–J4 / B1–B4 / emergency (`src/tram.js`) | in game |
| T3-P2 | The drive pedal chooses an **acceleration step**; the accelerator steps through the starting resistances. Its lamp going out under drive means the accelerator is out of the traction circuit. The number of steps is not given. | T3SUCS; ucebnice p. 4, 12 (by contrast with T3M); t3zr Q2852 | 4 drive notches | open: the step count |
| T3-P3 | On the T3M pedal depth sets a **target speed** (drive) or **brake force**, and must go deeper than on a T3; pressing both pedals at once is wrong. T3R.P: **7 drive and 7 brake steps**, drive depth sets the speed. | T3M, T3R.P; ucebnice p. 12, 16 | – | n/a (other variants) |
| T3-P4 | **Coasting is the technique**: avoid repeated drive presses that do not raise the speed; use momentum as much as possible (*jízda výběhem*). D 1/2 § 32.1 says the same for every driver. | T3SUCS; ucebnice p. 4 | coasting possible, not scored | candidate: score drive presses that gain no speed |
| T3-P5 | While coasting a T3SUCS draws a **"preparation" current of 40–90 A** (the motors run as dynamos ready for the dynamic brake), so its brake responds at once. A T3M coasts at 0 A and brakes later, with a longer stop. | T3SUCS, T3M; ucebnice p. 5, 11–12; t3zr Q2853; tech Q2863 | ammeter readouts exist (`src/tram.js` electrical readouts) | check the coasting reading |
| T3-P6 | **A fast change from drive to brake** makes the electrodynamic brake drop out and the mechanical brake come in early. The pause needed is not given. | T3SUCS; ucebnice p. 4 | force changes are rate-limited (drive +24 / −30 kN/s, brake +28 kN/s) | missing (a penalty or a weaker, harsher stop) |
| T3-P7 | Brake pedal order (*inferred*): released → electrodynamic range → **parked** (*zaparkováno*, the mechanical brake holds) → beyond it **track brake on the rear bogie** → pedal on the floor, **track brakes on both bogies**. | T3SUCS; ucebnice p. 5 | B1–B4 dynamic, emergency = full track brake + sand | partly (no parked position, no one-bogie stage) |
| T3-P8 | The **mechanical brake comes in by itself at 5–3 km/h**, where the dynamic brake cannot stop the car; switching control off applies it too. No brake depends on the 600 V supply. | T3SUCS; ucebnice p. 5 | the dynamic brake's fade below 2.5 m/s changes only the readouts | partly |
| T3-P9 | **Park the pedal at every stop**; on a gradient a released pedal lets the car roll (T3R.P; T3M parks on stage 2, stage 1 releases when the pedal is let go). D 1/2 § 34.3 requires "Zaparkováno" whenever standing. | T3R.P, T3M; ucebnice p. 12, 16; tech Q2866 | brake notch 2 is held while the doors are open; rollback (−30) when the brake is released on a hill | differs: a parked position to set by hand |
| T3-P10 | **Red emergency button** (*záchranná brzda*): mechanical + track brake + **bell** (and the dynamic brake if the pedal is parked); only with control on. | T3SUCS; ucebnice p. 5; t3zr/tech Q2855 | STOP (held) = emergency notch | partly (no bell, no mechanical brake) |
| T3-P11 | Brake lights come on with control on, while braking and with the pedal parked; the panel's mechanical-brake lamps "I", "BRZDY II", "VLAK" are lit while that brake is applied. | T3SUCS; ucebnice p. 7, 13 | – | missing |
| T3-P12 | No horn exists; the bell is the warning device. | all (no source mentions a horn) | bell (B) | in game |

## 4. Sanding (*pískovače*)

[T3SUCS] A lever pulled fully up opens the flaps by a spring and Bowden cable; sand falls **before
both wheels of the first axle**. The sand boxes sit under the first seats, right and left, and are
heated (ucebnice p. 6). [T3R.P] also electric sanders driven by the slip protection (ucebnice p. 18).
Game: hold A to sand, 90 s of sand, adhesion +0.1 (in game).

## 8. Two-car sets (*souprava 2×T3*)

- The second car's control switch is off; its lights (T3SUCS, T3M) are switched on that car (ucebnice p. 5, 7; t3zr Q2870); heating of the second car in 2×T3SU is set on the second car (t3zr Q2871).
- Mechanical-brake lamps on the leading car after applying drive: **"VLAK" alone** = a brake still on in car 2; "I" + "VLAK" = bogie 1 of car 1; "II" + "VLAK" = bogie 2 of car 1 (t3zr/tech Q4097, Q4100, Q4101).
- T3SUCS emergency drive (*havarijní pojezd*) switches off all traction-fed equipment of a car, so it only works in a set; the defective car still needs its mechanical and track brakes, control, motor-generator, battery and the multiple-unit cable (ucebnice p. 8).

Game: both cars motored, forces scale with the cars; only the first car's pantograph works the
contacts (in game).

## 11. Open questions for the user

1. How many drive and brake positions does the T3SUCS pedal have (the game uses 4 + 4)?
2. How is the brake pedal parked and unparked, and where is "parked" between the dynamic range and the track brake?
3. How long must a T3SUCS coast between drive and brake to keep the dynamic brake (T3-P6)?
4. With the working contact, which direction does current set, and how far before the switch is the contact (T3-W5)?
5. Does a T3SUCS block traction while a door is open (T3-D2)?
6. Does the game's T3SUCS have door-request or stop-request buttons? D 1/2 § 27.14 d makes a train **without** them stop at every request stop ([rules-d1-2.md](rules-d1-2.md) D12-S3).
