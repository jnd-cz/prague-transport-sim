This hybrid contains assets from two sources:

1. Base body, interior and cab
This work is based on "Tatra T3" (https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/tatra-t3-2c08e2e39ace45569c45a2fdbb4052c0) by SimonStrikesBack (https://sketchfab.com/SimonStrikesBack) licensed under CC-BY-4.0 (http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

2. Collector, mirror arm, route-plate support, lamp geometry/textures and exterior visual reference
This work also incorporates the collector, mirror assembly, small route-plate support, lamp geometry and texture regions from "Tatra T3" (https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/tatra-t3-76aa08d7c06340fc89229551c45e3c3a) by conflig (https://sketchfab.com/conflig), licensed under CC BY-NC 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/).

Modifications: simplified repeated interior tubing; added rigid-node folding doors, an articulated Conflig collector with adjustable contact height, bogie and wheel pivots, emissive signal lenses, editable fleet/route-number decals; enlarged fleet numbers; adjusted paint colours; extracted and repacked lamp UV texture regions; aligned transferred lamps with the rigged body; narrowed door glass and frame apertures; added red brake lenses/emission, rear fleet number, editable mirror-arm route numbering and inverted rear route colours; raised the rear route card and added front destination, white-on-black run number and side route/text cards based on user-supplied reference photos. Seated the front/rear cards at the lower window frames, moved the forward side card to the second window, removed left-side cards, and lowered the protruding dashboard shelf tip to clear the front card. Squared the front destination card, fitted the run-number card to the right wraparound pane, cleared the dashboard and tilted the upper route plate upward by ten degrees. No reference-photo pixels are embedded.
