Changes: the four 4096 px PBR maps were reduced to one 2048 px colour map with the ambient
occlusion baked in (normal and metallic maps dropped); the glTF was trimmed accordingly.
The game adds its own route number boxes over the painted ones.

Because of the NonCommercial term this model must not be used in any commercial release of the
game; the built-in procedural model (src/models.js) has no such restriction.
