The T3 cabin the passengers use (assets/passengers/t3-cabin.json, #249)

Generated by tools/cabin.py --game, never edited by hand: the passenger lab's cabin preparation (tools/cabin/, from the
project's own asset lab "street-life-boarding" v14, 2026-10-27; AGPL v3 like the code) run on

- the detailed hybrid T3 model, assets/t3h/tatra-t3-hybrid-nc.glb (its SHA-256 is the file's assetSha256): the seats, the
  doors and their entry steps, the floor and the cab are measured from its geometry. See assets/t3h/LICENSE.txt for its
  sources: "Tatra T3" by SimonStrikesBack (CC BY 4.0) and "Tatra T3" by Jakub Vaja (conflig) (CC BY-NC 4.0);
- the pedestrian figure's body outline, assets/people/person-near.glb (the project's own, AGPL v3);

with the standing places of a packing search (tools/cabin/packing-search.js; its options are in the file: 16 headings on a
2 cm grid, 3000 moves, seed 1): 24 seats and 81 standing places, 105 people shown a car (the user, 2026-11-25, #249). The
floor behind the cab's partition by the front door is open floor (the user, the same day); the search ran on the floor
before that fix (the same search on the corrected floor packs 80), each of its places checked on the corrected floor
(tools/cabin.py --packing-from).
