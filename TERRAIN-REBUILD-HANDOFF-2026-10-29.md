# Terrain and street rebuild: Výtoň study and handoff

**Date:** 2026-10-29  
**Status:** research and source preparation; work in progress, not implemented in the game. Awaiting original development checkout, world-build tools, and further source checks.  
**Scope:** road and sidewalk waviness, terrain source selection, level separation, and a pilot rebuild. This supplements `TRACK-DATA-DECISION-2026-10-27.md`, `BUILD-TOOLS-HANDOFF.md`, and the historical `PLAN.md`; it does not supersede the agreed track-centreline source preference.

## Practical conclusion

Use a composite world, preserving the meaning and level of each surface. The Prague 1 m terrain raster is a promising base for surrounding ground. Test the current technical map's surveyed geometry, points and surface types as constraints for streets, sidewalks, kerbs, walls, tracks and other built features. Do not mix a quay, street, bridge or tunnel merely because they overlap in plan. Establish source currency, height reference, quality and matching before adopting any geometry or height as truth.

This study does not establish the cause of all visible arching or prove that a new dataset alone fixes it. Existing historical notes identify the earlier offline weighted-mean street fit as a source of arches on slopes; a plane fit reduced the reported counts but waviness remained. The browser also modifies surfaces in `src/world.js` `flushDtm`, and the deployed code's comments record a previous case in which the correction transferred survey noise into waves. Exactly how much each step contributes at the target locations remains unmeasured. No game or build code was changed by this study.

## Sources and status

1. **IPR Prague lidar-derived terrain, 1 m GeoTIFF.** Acquired float32 1 m crop in S-JTSK / Krovak East North (EPSG:5514). Source processing metadata records cubic resampling to 1 m in July 2025; a 25 cm intermediate is named but its actual resolution and public availability are unconfirmed. The downloaded TIFF's explicit vertical datum and local capture date were not established. IPR describes a 2023 lidar-derived model and a 1 m published raster: https://geoportalpraha.cz/data-a-sluzby/clanky-a-projekty/3D-model/3D-model-dtm . Use as a candidate base for terrain, not automatically as an authoritative rail, bridge or curb height.
2. **Current regional DTM, Prague 2 ZPS, state 30 September 2026.** Original full district archives included in the separate sample archive under `current_zps_praha2/`: ZPS_KR construction/drawing, ZPS_PB detailed points, and ZPS_OPL derived surface polygons. XML headers state JVF 1.4.3; dataset release date is not the individual observation date. Downloaded and ZIP-integrity-checked; **not clipped to the study area, geometrically compared, or integrated**. Check quality attributes, geometry, timestamps, vertical reference and licensing. Official data route: https://portal.dtm-praha-sck.cz/poskytovani-dat/zpusoby-poskytovani-prazskych-dat-dtm . Future builds must consider the scheduled JVF version transition in the existing build handoff.
3. **Archived Prague technical map (DTMP) lines and points.** Extracted for the Výtoň vicinity, as original Esri JSON in EPSG:5514 with numeric Z and separate WGS84 display GeoJSON. Query returned 3,169 line features (including 50 tram-axis segments, 493 broad road/infrastructure lines, 383 greenery/sidewalk lines and three vertical retaining-wall lines) and 1,299 point features including 1,016 code 1950 elevation points from as-built documentation. Server-side transformed envelopes include some records outside the WGS84 rectangle; line features are not clipped. Class codes are broad; source timestamps are not necessarily survey dates. The GeoJSON Z values are source heights, **not WGS84 ellipsoidal heights**. No queried terrain slope-edge codes 42105–42108 occurred in this sample. Query sources: https://gs-pub.praha.eu/arcgis/rest/services/dtmp/dtmp/MapServer/9 and /10. Reconcile with current ZPS before reuse.
4. **ČÚZK DMR 5G.** Sample includes 6,635 model points in LAZ from Praha 7-2 and the game's existing 2 m DMR-derived terrain samples. The LAS header did not contain a readable CRS; source product uses S-JTSK/Bpv. Model points are not raw IPR lidar returns. Useful as an independent product comparison or a fallback outside city coverage, subject to observation dates and surface type.
5. **Track sources.** Preserve the prior agreement in `TRACK-DATA-DECISION-2026-10-27.md`: prefer current DTM tram centrelines where records are current, usable and consistent; use older technical map and OSM as reviewed gap candidates. Avoid duplicate or obsolete alignments and preserve switches, topology and per-source provenance. Track elevation selection is separate.

## Measurements and limits

Study square: 360 × 360 m in the legacy line 17 local frame, approximately Výtoň and the railway bridge. The comparison between the stored game 2 m terrain and the IPR 1 m raster used 30,589 paired samples: median game minus IPR +0.105 m; 90th-percentile absolute difference 0.790 m. Large differences are concentrated around river edge and structures. This includes water, grade-separated surfaces and terrain whose meaning may differ; it is not a road-error or source-accuracy estimate.

At 894 archived code-1950 points inside the geographic rectangle, survey minus nearest IPR raster cell median +0.014 m and 90th-percentile absolute difference 0.095 m; range −3.274 to +0.543 m. At 196 archived tram-axis vertices: median +0.036 m, 90th-percentile absolute 0.072 m. Observations are spatially correlated and potentially share provenance; samples are not independent ground truth. At 20 railway-axis vertices the median survey-minus-raster was +4.833 m, consistent with a higher structure; the exact interpretation still needs site review. Values do not independently validate datums, provenance or absolute accuracy.

Legacy local-to-WGS84 conversion approximates latitude/longitude metres per degree and can affect matching; raster values are nearest cells and game terrain uses its original triangle interpolation. Vertical datums and local capture dates are not confirmed. Do not silently equate heights or apply centimetre-level acceptance to these results.

## Recommended rebuild implementation

- **Coordinates and provenance:** use the agreed city-wide S-JTSK frame and local Prague-centred rendering origin. Define and test exact horizontal transformations and vertical reference conversions offline; distinguish Bpv, EVRS and unknown heights. Retain provider, feature ID, acquisition/update dates where available, flags, source CRS, height reference and processing versions.
- **Streets / infrastructure:** test current ZPS surveyed lines, points and classed polygons as constraints to reconstruct road, sidewalk, track, kerb and retaining-wall geometry. Classify and explicitly represent bridge deck, underpass, upper road, quay, water and terrain. Fit within a physical surface/level and do not smooth across structural breaklines; do not blindly union outdated records. Preserve distinct track centres, connections and switches and integrate track, road, rail, vehicle and terrain elevations together.
- **Natural terrain:** use 1 m IPR raster as a candidate general base for Prague; assess DMR 5G as fallback where appropriate. Incorporate verified surveyed slope tops/feet, walls, riverbanks and terrain edges where available. Prototype an adaptive mesh or constrained TIN offline with more detail at real discontinuities and fewer vertices in smooth regions. The INSPIRE GML inspected here provides no source triangles or breaklines; triangulation would be a derived product and cannot add missing accuracy.
- **Browser runtime:** favor checked, route-independent compiled geometry and tiling. Existing runtime profile smoothing, `carveCorridor`, `flushDtm`, rail/bed offsets and bridge adjustments can change displayed results; measure them jointly before disabling or replacing any one step. Separate bare-earth terrain from structured, multi-level surfaces.
- **Testing / accuracy:** use both geometry and rendered/simulation contact tests; report horizontal and vertical differences, gradients and edge continuity separately. Confirm data licences and required attribution; IPR guidance says “datový podklad © IPR Praha”; ČÚZK usage terms also apply.
